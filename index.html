About

I am a meteorologist working on atmospheric turbulence. As a Research Assistant at the Institute of Geophysics and Meteorology, University of Cologne, I study the influence of submesoscale motions on turbulence intermittency in the stable Arctic boundary layer in the Arctic. I combine observations from the MOSAiC expedition in the Arctic with the ICON model to develop stochastic turbulence parameterisations for the stable boundary layer in km-scale climate models.

I received my PhD in Meteorology from Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), India. My thesis was on turbulence in the tropical troposphere, using the 205 MHz Stratosphere–Troposphere (S-T) wind profiler radar at Kochi. My work spans estimating the turbulent kinetic energy dissipation rate from the S-T radar, its statistics, and seasonal variability. I used the machine learning algorithm XGBoost to develop an ML model using the data, combined with explainable AI SHAP to analyse the feature importance of the trained supervised model in optimising the dissipation rate values based on SHAP value theory.
